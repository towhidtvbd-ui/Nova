Nova Snake

Collect food, avoid walls and your tail, and chase a high score.

Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to move, P to pause, R to restart.

How to Play

  • Eat regular food for points and growth.
  • Collect power food for bonus points and temporary slow motion.
  • Every 5 foods increases level and speed.
  • You lose a life on collision. Game ends at 0 lives.
